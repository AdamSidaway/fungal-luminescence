# Query set provenance

Source: Kotlobay et al. 2018, PNAS 115(50):12728, doi:10.1073/pnas.1803615115, SI Datasets S1-S4.

**There are no GenBank accessions for these four enzymes.** The paper
deposits genomes and transcriptomes under BioProject PRJNA476325, but the
individual pathway genes appear only as FASTA in the SI, keyed by species
name. Shakhova et al. 2024 likewise gives Addgene plasmids and a figshare
DOI rather than sequence accessions, and Palkina et al. 2023 states
'Not applicable' for data availability. The query set therefore cannot be
cited by accession and is checksummed instead.

The SI datasets are coding nucleotide, not protein, and are translated here.
